## Intent

Connect the deterministic orchestration core to supported Codex session and
managed-worktree capabilities, then prove dispatch, integration, recovery, and
cleanup through the complete campaign path.

## Acceptance criteria

- The adapter supplies exact live session and managed-worktree observations to
  the reconciler.
- Repository status maps the top-level worktree's active campaign and plans to
  its bound coordinator, assignments, sessions, and worktrees.
- Managed worker invocations are detected through authenticated host ownership;
  read-only operations re-root to their owner and mutations proxy securely or
  fail closed.
- An idle worker is reused safely; absent idle capacity creates exactly one new
  worker for one claimed plan.
- Worker completion triggers evidence-backed rebase and fast-forward merge
  transitions without trusting conversational completion alone.
- Integrated workers are archived and their managed worktrees removed, while
  partial cleanup remains retryable and non-idle.
- Automated and live UAT pass against the same versioned core contracts.

## Scope and exclusions

Execution begins only after S01 freezes and approves the core action protocol.
This sprint uses only supported Codex host capabilities. It does not reverse
engineer application storage, invoke private APIs, or add an alternate
scheduler inside the adapter.

## Global contracts to preserve

- The deterministic core owns every scheduling and transition decision.
- The host adapter is a thin observer and actuator.
- One top-level worktree has one authorized coordinator and at most one active
  campaign.
- Distinct top-level worktrees may have independent coordinators and campaigns;
  no broader machine or Git-common-directory lock is introduced.
- Cleanup cannot precede proof that the exact worker revision is integrated.
