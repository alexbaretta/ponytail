## Intent

Reconcile the approved requirement, then implement campaign inventory,
lifecycle listing and activation, explicit orchestration selection, and shared-
worktree safety.

## Acceptance criteria

- Multiple active campaigns are valid repository-wide inventory.
- `campaign list` defaults to active campaigns and filters each normalized
  campaign status.
- `campaign activate <plan-name>` recursively resolves the campaign root and
  idempotently moves a pending campaign to active work without broken links.
- Explicit commands operate on the selected campaign; ambiguous implicit
  commands identify all candidates and do not mutate state.
- One session may coordinate several campaigns in one worktree, with campaign-
  exact authorization and ambiguous composer enqueue rejection.
- Cross-campaign advances serialize around their shared integration worktree.
- Policy, CLI help, UAT, traceability, and tests match the implementation.

## Scope and exclusions

Exact paths are frozen in the reviewed tasklet graph. Independent coordinator
sessions in one worktree, new campaign membership schemas, and external
mutations remain excluded.

## Global contracts to preserve

- Existing physical campaign metadata, ledger, status, action, worker-binding,
  and coordinator-binding readers remain immutable.
- The changed repository-inventory semantics use a new immutable physical
  version; older inventory readers remain exact.
- Selected-campaign validation remains isolated from unrelated defects.
- Fast-forward-only integration and authenticated worker ownership remain.
- Distinct top-level worktrees remain independent.
