## Objective and scope

Produce a reviewable contract and retirement matrix answering Q1-Q6. Drafting
this sprint does not authorize implementing those answers. Keep the discussion
record's proposed details distinct from stakeholder decisions.

## Draft work packages

1. **Graph and adequacy contract.** Enumerate physical versions, identity sources,
   structural versus semantic completeness, test selection identity, and expected
   diagnostics. Define examples with multiple obligations per requirement and
   multiple acceptances/tests, including an uncovered acceptance hidden by a
   requirement-level test. Resolve Q1.
2. **Validation boundary.** Design a compact tag payload and focused/final run
   lifecycle, actual command-result capture, exact revision/review references,
   input freshness, failed runs, tag corrections and no source-tree writes.
   Distinguish test results from approval. Resolve Q2.
3. **Campaign and host boundary.** Inventory every current CLI/MCP operation;
   classify keep/edit/retire/historical-reader with callers and configuration.
   Define only the operations needed for graph/status/acceptance/tagging. Native
   tools own sessions; do not add a Ponytail host-session API. Resolve Q3/Q5.
4. **Monitoring/adoption contract.** Define timestamps, disturbance/scope identity,
   restart and stale-result behavior, minimum retained operational state, and
   bounded active/historical migration. Resolve Q4/Q6.
5. **Activation preparation.** Map existing requirement/architecture/UAT owners,
   explicit supersessions, required issue approvals, and every CM outcome's
   future test. Prepare exact atomic tasklet paths and dependency graphs; record
   decisions before seeking execution approval and starting checks.

## Owning surfaces and acceptance

Inspect `docs/campaign-management.md`, the 17 source skills and shared glossary;
`src/rolling-*`, `src/swarm-*`, campaign census/plan-input/worktree callers;
`cli/ponytail`, `src/cli-help.ts`, `ponytail-mcp/`, `scripts/project-index.sql`,
traceability configuration/checkers and `versioned-data-contracts.json`.

Acceptance: the proposed graph/tag/state/CLI contracts are explicit, their
authority is recorded, historical interpretations are preserved, and later
sprints can be decomposed without inventing public behavior. No CLI removal,
live DB migration, host mutation, or source-skill activation occurs here under
planning-only authority. Validation: links, schemas and dependency checks;
execution-readiness remains false until all required gates are satisfied.
