## Physical V1 contract

One explicit obligation document has exactly schemaVersion, scopeId, baselines,
obligations, certificates and checkpoints. The latest baseline supplies id,
predecessorId, authority (references and authorized assessor IDs), requirements (id/path/digest), obligationIds and inventoryIds.
Each obligation has id (OBL-positive integer), category (T1-T4/C1-C2/gate),
requirementIds, responsibilityId, condition, criterion, dependsOn, acceptanceGroups, supersedes (null or earlier ID),
execution (sprintId/taskletIds/plannedPaths/operations/selection/target). Certificates have id,
obligationIds, inputs (path/digest), artifacts, assessor, authority, adequate,
reason, disposition, evidence and inventoryIds. Execution evidence has command,
selection, target, revision, dirty, startedAt, completedAt, outcome, skips, failures,
inputs, outputPath and finalization. Target has identity, profile and verification reference.
Disposition is null or exact responsibilityId/reason/authority; artifact references
are repository-relative paths. All number tokens must be safe nonnegative integers. No time-to-live or inferred scope.

Checkpoints have id, predecessorId, baselineId, kind, selectedIds, registeredIds, certificateIds,
invalidatedIds, before, after, preparation, reason and assessor. An initial
observation starts history without claiming productive progress. Preparation names
obligationId, operations and deliverable; repeated identical completed preparation
on unchanged state is rejected. Prefix immutability preserves accepted history.
registeredIds records membership additions per checkpoint so historical vectors
remain stable when discovery appends current baseline membership. Initial and
rebaseline observations register their entire known membership; publication
rejects hidden unregistered IDs. Satisfaction is derived from replay, current input digests and adequate evidence.
Inventory certificates enumerate the obligations exposed by their assessment.
Absence of a declared inventory marker cannot certify global completion.

The V1 reader is exported through ObligationDocumentReaders (V1); the manifest declares V1 as the current writer version. The API is independently testable; semantic adequacy remains
an attributed assessment rather than machine interpretation of assertions.
