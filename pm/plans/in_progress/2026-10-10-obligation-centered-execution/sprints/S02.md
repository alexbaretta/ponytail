Implement the approved durable record, certificate references, transition
validation, and obligation-first readiness selection. Prove lexicographic
comparison, earliest-coordinate ordering, finite neutral preparation records,
honest disturbances/rebaselines, exact scope and dependency preservation, and
historical contract compatibility. Semantic review remains distinct from tools.

focused behavioral tests; build an atomic graph only after S01 settles contracts.
No second scheduler, environment adapter assumptions, or semantic auto-approval.
Execution dependency on S01 must be recorded when execution metadata is created.
