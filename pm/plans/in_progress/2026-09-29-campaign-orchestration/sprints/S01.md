## Intent

Establish repository-wide campaign inventory plus deterministic dependency,
assignment, reconciliation, and one-step transition contracts independently of
Codex host actuation.

## Acceptance criteria

- A latest-write campaign metadata version authors direct plan dependencies and
  preserves exact V1 reading.
- Repository-wide validation classifies every configured plan, permits zero or
  one active campaign per top-level worktree, reports every conflicting
  candidate without guessing, and keeps explicit selected-campaign validation
  isolated from unrelated defects.
- No-input reporting identifies active campaigns, active member plans, invalid
  managed plans, and permitted unmanaged legacy plans through a versioned
  contract.
- A versioned operational ledger atomically prevents duplicate active plan,
  session, and worktree assignments.
- Status deterministically identifies ready plans, active and idle workers,
  rebase and merge readiness, cleanup state, and actionable contradictions.
- One-step transitions are idempotent and recover after interruption without
  losing or duplicating work.
- Git readiness permits only verified fast-forward integration.

## Scope and exclusions

The reviewed graph freezes every implementation, test, generated,
configuration, and PM path. This sprint does not create, message, archive, or
remove a real Codex session or managed worktree and does not use a private host
API.

## Global contracts to preserve

- Campaign lifecycle and membership retain their existing canonical owners.
- Direct dependencies are authored once; reverse edges and readiness are
  derived.
- Operational assignments never enter Git project-management records.
- Existing V1 readers remain immutable.
- `campaign validate --all` and every `campaign report` mode remain read-only
  census commands.
- One top-level worktree has at most one active campaign and coordinator;
  distinct top-level worktrees remain independent.
