## Feature S01-F01: Campaign census contracts and CLI

Acceptance: the installed `ponytail` entrypoint validates and reports only the
campaign containing the supplied plan, through exact versioned contracts and
the canonical sprint/tasklet validators, without mutation.

### [ ] Tasklet S01-F01-T01: Define the management configuration reader

Create V1 project-management configuration and its exact reader. Normalize the
management root, plan root, ordered lifecycle directories and roles, and legacy
flat-layout flag. Reject unknown keys, unsafe paths, duplicate directories, and
non-bijective lifecycle roles.

Focused proof: exact valid, malformed, unknown-key, and unsafe-path fixtures.

### [ ] Tasklet S01-F01-T02: Define plan metadata and campaign discovery

Parse exactly one `ponytail-plan-campaign` block for selected members, follow
parent backlinks, derive descendants through deterministic scanning, and fully
validate only the resulting campaign. Unrelated malformed campaigns remain out
of scope.

Focused proof: root/leaf equivalence, missing and ambiguous parents, cycles,
duplicate member IDs, unrelated invalid campaigns, and legacy records.

Focused proof: valid mixed status census, selector failure, closure failure,
and zero-tasklet percentage.

unknown output keys, and repository identity fields.
