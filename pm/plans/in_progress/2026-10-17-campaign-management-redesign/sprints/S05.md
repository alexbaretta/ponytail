## Resulting skill and tool set

Replace `swarm` with one short `campaign-management` skill. Describe complete
worker assignments, local adversarial review, worker-owned rebase/tests/tags,
coordinator capacity/unblocking, serial fast-forward delivery, vector reporting,
instability and independent final QA. Use native host session/worktree controls
and Git; do not replace the swarm CLI with another orchestrator.

Rewrite `plan-execution` and `worktree-lifecycle` where their existing structure
would preserve the old bureaucracy. Keep ordinary planning, canonical vocabulary,
unfinished-work preservation and project resource cleanup. Remove redundant
instructions rather than scattering the same protocol among skills. Edit the
other affected skills only to align their actual ownership and references.

Delete exported `parallel-plan-scheduler` and `qa-swarm`. Retain useful QA
selection/isolation guidance in its existing QA owner; do not automatically add
another campaign reference or preserve historical scheduler instructions.

Remove swarm dispatch and obsolete campaign allocation, lease, queue, review
and publication protocols from CLI/MCP, help, permissions and registry. Delete
unused implementations/tests. Preserve unrelated Git/rebase, search, indexing,
project operations and necessary historical inspection. No new compatibility
facade or migration subsystem.

Regenerate host copies/manifests/adapters with existing generators. Verify
installation removes retired owned files while preserving user files. Use
focused tests for retained tool behavior and distribution consistency; real
native Gwen operation establishes the campaign behavior.
