## Feature S01-F02: Explicit owner and coordinator persistence

### [IN_PROGRESS] Tasklet S01-F02-T01: Persist exclusive coordinator bindings

Scope: `src/swarm-store.ts`, `scripts/project-index.sql`,
`tests/swarm-store.integration.test.ts`, their generated runtime and project
configuration/traceability mappings. OBL-1 and OBL-2 own the conditions.
Enroll canonical physical user checkouts and reject managed checkout enrollment.
Use one owner row to enforce coordinator exclusivity; reject stale unbinding
without deleting campaign assignments or checkouts. Enrollment and low-level
binding are independently tested before exposing the native CLI adapter.

Focused real PostgreSQL proof passes: two simultaneous binds yield one winner;
same-session binding is idempotent; a stale unbind cannot remove the winner;
unbinding preserves worker assignments and physical checkout state. The test was
added after the initial implementation draft, so it does not establish the
cross-session-effects skill's failing-first gate. Native identity validation,
explicit membership adoption and CLI exposure remain pending; this tasklet and
OBL-2 are not discharged by low-level storage proof.

Adversarial review identified retained managed checkouts without DB rows and
missing/replaced enrolled roots as admission gaps. New regressions failed on
each missing rejection before its fix. Enrollment now reads the canonical
configured physical pool and excludes managed paths even after reset, under
the existing project advisory fence. Coordinator binding rechecks the retained
physical Git identity before its transaction commits.
