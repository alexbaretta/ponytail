# Obligation-centered execution

[Back to requirements index](index.md)

**Redesign supersession:** For explicitly adopted redesigned campaigns, the
[campaign-management contract](campaign-management.md) supersedes the
six-coordinate progress model, direct requirement-only test coverage, mandatory
unit-and-integration quotas, tracked passing-evidence ledgers and Ponytail swarm
session/queue orchestration described below. Historical records and readers
retain their original meanings. This notice does not claim adoption or completion.

**Atomicity amendment approval/source:** On 2026-10-10 the stakeholder directed:
"record your definition and make it normative: requirements must be expressed
in terms of atomic obligations. Similarly, acceptances must be expressed in
terms of the atomic obligations, and so on." This approves the preceding
definition of the smallest independently verifiable functional condition that
can be integrated as a coherent change. It does not waive existing scope,
evidence, authority or historical-identity requirements.

This requirement includes these atomic policy obligations, defined here and
referenced by their stable anchors. The glossary owns the shared atomicity
definition; the owning skills implement its application.

### OBL-GLOSSARY-ATOMICITY

**Condition:** Ponytail's canonical glossary defines and applies obligation
atomicity consistently across rules and skills.
**Discharge criterion:** Source/generated parity and review establish one
canonical definition with meaningful outcome, independent verdict, coherent
integration and useful-subdivision criteria; coding fragments and file/test/time
counts are not substituted for functional atomicity.

### OBL-GLOSSARY-REQUIREMENTS

**Condition:** The requirements skill requires each approved requirement
to state or reference its identified atomic obligations.
**Discharge criterion:** Review establishes that obligation conditions retain
all approved behavior and provenance, with canonical conditions and discharge
criteria referenced instead of duplicated or silently weakened.

### OBL-GLOSSARY-ACCEPTANCES

**Condition:** The acceptance skill requires each acceptance to identify the
atomic obligations and observations it demonstrates.
**Discharge criterion:** Review establishes explicit condition and Arc/Step
links and complete-obligation assessment, allowing joint demonstrations while
rejecting partial proof presented as complete obligation or requirement proof.

### OBL-GLOSSARY-EXECUTION

**Condition:** Planning, assignment, artifact assessment and certificates use
the same canonical atomic obligation identities throughout delivery.
**Discharge criterion:** Review establishes linked scope, prerequisites,
responsibility boundaries and proof without a second condition registry;
work-count reporting separates atomic delivery from inventory and final gates,
and historical decomposition requires an explicit successor baseline.

Acceptances: [GLOSSARY-ATOMICITY](../uat/obligation-execution.md#glossary-atomicity).

**Approval:** The stakeholder approved the obligation execution plan, design,
glossary and named CHNGs with "Approved. Go." on 2026-10-10.

**Source:** approved plan `2026-10-10-obligation-centered-execution`,

`2026-10-10-CHNG-obligation_centered_execution` and
`2026-10-10-CHNG-repair_plan_starting_gate`.

Ponytail must reuse unchanged relevant input evidence and reject stale, failing, skipped or wrong-target proof.

For new campaign certificates, record the tested Git revision and relevant
tracked input paths, not file-hash manifests. Use Git comparisons to detect
committed, staged and unstaged changes, including new untracked source files
inside an assessed directory scope. Reject inputs absent from the tested
revision. Historical hash records remain readable; new campaign acceptance
must reject them. Retained command logs may be ignored files and are evidence
outputs rather than tracked source inputs.

2026-10-12 after the certificate-history repair.
